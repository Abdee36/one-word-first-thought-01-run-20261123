## The word

**borborygmus** — the rumbling, growling sound of the stomach.

Surprising, vivid and specific: everyone has felt it, and the word itself
growls. One word, 11 characters, no explanation needed.

Why this word and not a pretty one: the field is already full of
`petrichor`, `apricity` and `afterglow` — words everyone reaches for when
asked for a beautiful word. `borborygmus` is the opposite direction: a
bodily, onomatopoeic, oddly musical word a reader would not have guessed,
that still calls up a clear feeling in a single glance.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the single word to
`/output/entry.txt`, after a self-check that it is exactly one word of
1–24 characters with no whitespace (the bounty gate).

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
# borborygmus
```
